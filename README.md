# rogic
- dnd based roguelike rpg. your chars stats, bg, personality, relations affect how you experience the world. and if u die, you restart. 
- i made this bcuz i like dnd and rpgs. mainly games where your choices actually matter, and also matter in actions and the story.
- small demo of a huge concept-game. Concepts-
- The speaking choices will be based on your personality, and there are alotta clans, which makes characters speak differently to u. after that, thrs a wide range of weapons, armor, and accessories.
- Sub classes, Bosses. Keep in mind, this is an open world game. There will be different dnd aspects such as rolling for stuff like stealth, persuasion and all which are affected by stats.
- Different magical items, dungeons, alignments, factions, choices remembered by npcs, dating, crafting, smart enemies, languages and much more.
- AI WAS USED to debug some code (<15%).
